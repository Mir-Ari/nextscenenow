Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

This policy explains what data NSN Poster, the internal publishing tool of Next Scene Now, handles.

1. Data we use

When we connect our own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube account, the platform gives us an access token and basic account information (such as the account name and ID). We use these only to upload our own videos and to check that an upload succeeded.

2. Data we do not collect

The tool has no other users. It does not collect, read or store personal data about viewers, followers or anyone else.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are kept in an encrypted system keychain on our own computer. They are never published, sold or shared with third parties.

4. Sharing

Videos are sent only to the platform they are published on. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Removal

We can revoke the tool's access at any time in each platform's settings, which makes the stored tokens useless. We then delete them.

6. Contact

Send a direct message to @nextscenenow100 on Instagram.
